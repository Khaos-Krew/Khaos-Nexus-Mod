# Khaos Nexus 1.2 audio

All 1.2 sounds under `assets/khaosnexus/sounds/king/` and `assets/khaosnexus/sounds/music/` are original works
synthesised procedurally by `tools/gen_sounds.py` (numpy additive/FM synthesis, filtered noise, convolution reverb).
No samples, loops or third-party recordings are used.
