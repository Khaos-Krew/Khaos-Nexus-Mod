# Khaos Nexus

**Break the gates. Climb the Spire. Weave the Khaos.**

Khaos Nexus is a NeoForge RPG mod for Minecraft 1.21.1. Ranked dungeon gates open across your world, an endless tower waits to be climbed, and every player chooses a class, carries a Soulbound Codex, and grows their Khaos Rank. It's built to drop into big packs like All the Mods 10, and its difficulty and loot scale to whatever modpack it's in.

> **Status:** in planning. Development starts in mid-October 2026. ATM10 (1.21.1) comes first, with an ATM11 port once it's stable. It will be released on CurseForge.

---

## Features (planned)

### Nexus Gates
- Ranked gates from **E to S** open near players a few times a day across the server, and Gate Keys open them too.
- Each gate leads to its own dungeon instance with a random theme, mobs scaled to your pack, and a boss at the end.
- You can enter a gate solo if it's at or below your hunter rank. Higher-ranked gates need a party.
- A gate stays open for **7 Minecraft days**, and entering takes a **Gate Permit** bought from the Gate Board. In the last half of day 6, permits are no longer needed and anyone can enter.
- An uncleared gate **breaks** and spills its monsters into the world. Breaks never happen in claimed land.
- **Sealed Gates** are rare. They lock the party in and raise the gate one rank.

### Raids
- Boss raids for parties of up to **5 players**, using FTB Teams or the mod's built-in party system.
- Boss health scales with party size, and every player gets their own loot.
- Loot has a weekly lockout, but players can keep running raids to help their friends.

### Classes
**Tank · Assassin · Caster · Ranger · Lifebinder · Berserker · Summoner · Necromancer**
- You can have one class equipped at a time, and each class has 6 abilities.
- Classes unlock through class trials, rare sigils found in gates, and level milestones.
- Swapping classes is free, with a cooldown.
- Summoners call elemental beasts, and Necromancers raise slain mobs as temporary minions.

### Khaos Weaving
- An original magic system built around your **Soulbound Codex**. A rare few players also get a second book, the **Twin Codex**.
- Each player gets a random element, and a rare shard can reroll it:
  - **Common:** Fire, Water, Wind, Earth
  - **Uncommon:** Lightning, Ice, Plant
  - **Rare:** Light, Shadow
  - **Ultra-rare:** Khaos

### Techniques
- Weapon skills for each weapon type, and your class makes some of them stronger.

### The Nexus Spire
- An endless, randomly generated tower with checkpoints every 10 floors.
- The first party to clear a floor gets the full rewards. Later clears pay less, which keeps the climb competitive.
- Players can join a friend's run and earn loot, but they don't unlock checkpoints they haven't reached themselves.
- Seasons reset only when a server admin starts a new one.

### Progression
- **Khaos Rank** is a player level separate from vanilla XP. It caps at 100, and then players can prestige.
- E-to-S **hunter ranks** go up as your Khaos Rank grows.
- Achievements earn titles you can show off, like *Spire Climber* and *Gate Breaker*.
- Loot tiers match the gate ranks, from E to S.

### And more
- A holographic floating-panel HUD.
- An in-game guide book, plus Ponder scenes that show how things work.
- Optional FTB Quests chapters for the mod, plus side chapters for supported companion mods.
- An optional Discord webhook for gate breaks, raid clears and Spire records.
- A PvP arena in a later update.

## Compatibility (planned)
Everything here is optional, and the mod runs without any of these installed.
- **Apotheosis / Apothic Attributes:** class and Codex affixes, plus new attributes for magic and summons.
- **Relics, Artifacts, Curios**
- **Ars Nouveau:** the main magic partner, with deeper integration planned in the **Ars Nexus** add-on.
- **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks:** light support.
- **FTB Teams, FTB Chunks, FTB Quests**
- **Parry mods:** when one is installed, the Tank uses that mod's parry and adds its class bonuses on top.

Server owners can tune gate rates, scaling, loot, minion caps and more in the config and datapacks.
