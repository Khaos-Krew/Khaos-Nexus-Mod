# Khaos Nexus

A NeoForge mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (built for All the Mods 10 first, ATM11 later), with Nexus Gates and raids, the endless Nexus Spire, eight classes, Khaos Weaving magic, and weapon Techniques. All of it scales to fit the modpack it's in.

**Status:** in planning. Development starts in mid-October 2026.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Khaos-Krew/Khaos-Nexus-Mod/issues/new/choose).
